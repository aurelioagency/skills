# Design truth — Reel 9:16

Concept angle: la misma introducción se despliega como un objeto vertical táctil: copy arriba, prueba/sistema en el centro y folder-origen abajo.

## Canvas and safety

- Canvas `1080×1920`.
- Essential text: `x=72–900`, `y=210–1540`.
- Right rail `x=920–1080` and bottom `y=1600–1920` contain no indispensable copy.

## Palette

- Cream `#F4EFE3`.
- Ink `#111111`.
- Secondary ink `#151B24`.
- Coral `#E2987F`.
- Slate personalization `#748B9E`.
- Green only when intrinsic to the ChatGPT source asset.

## Typography

- Helvetica Bold: uppercase heroes, structural copy and platform names.
- Helvetica Regular: connectors, support copy and metadata.
- Shelley: expressive pivots only: `se vean`, `a mano?`, `LLEVA TIEMPO`, `adaptable`, `CERCANO` and `DE CERO.`.
- All exact font files are local under `assets/fonts`.

## Composition

- Focal element: one physical card, folder or platform system per beat.
- Copy remains in the upper safe zone.
- Proof/system remains centered; causal folder sits in the lower safe area.
- Negative space stays empty unless it serves hierarchy.

## Motion

- A: `card-morph-anchor`, `cursor-drag`, `waterfall-entry`, `nudge-curve`.
- B: `card-morph-anchor`, `center-outward-expansion`, `spring-pop-entrance`, `svg-path-draw`.
- C: `control-target-sync`, `theme-crossfade-morph`, `card-morph-anchor`, `waterfall-entry`.
- Camera coral→slate is a causal asset transformation, not an incidental crossfade.
- The opening text holds its position after entering; the group moves only as the scene exits.
- The opening uses no decorative coral rule and the camera proof card contains no duplicate `ASÍ`.

## Binding source reuse

- The Claude / ChatGPT / Gemini reveal reuses `skill-folder-v1.png`, `claude-window-v1.png`, `chatgpt-window-v1.png` and `gemini-window-v1.png`.
- The three platform cards expand from the folder tab into a phone-native fan; they are not a crop of the horizontal layout.
- The route map, card identities and transform state persist across the `00:20` production-block boundary.
- Card arrivals use smooth `power3.out` settles without bounce or elastic overshoot.
