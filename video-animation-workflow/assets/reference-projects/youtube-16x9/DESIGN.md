# Design truth — YouTube 16:9

Concept angle: la introducción se desmonta frente al espectador y vuelve a ensamblarse como la prueba del workflow que se entrega.

## Canvas and safety

- Canvas `1920×1080`.
- Essential-copy margin: `96 px` left/right, `72 px` top/bottom.
- Persistent paper layer: `warm_cream_paper_background.png`.

## Palette

- Cream `#F4EFE3`.
- Ink `#111111`.
- Secondary ink `#151B24`.
- Coral `#E2987F`.
- Slate personalization `#748B9E`.
- Green only when intrinsic to the ChatGPT source asset.

## Typography

- Helvetica Bold: uppercase heroes, structural copy and platform names.
- Helvetica Regular: connectors, support copy and metadata.
- Shelley: expressive pivots only: `se vean`, `a mano?`, `LLEVA TIEMPO`, `adaptable`, `CERCANO` and `DE CERO.`.
- All exact font files are local under `assets/fonts`; no browser substitutes are part of the approved composition.

## Composition

- Focal element: one physical card, folder or platform system per beat.
- Edge anchor: large native copy aligned left.
- Supporting detail: one purposeful route, label group or control couple.
- Background: warm paper with restrained grain; no filler.

## Motion

- A: `card-morph-anchor`, `cursor-drag`, `waterfall-entry`, `nudge-curve`.
- B: `card-morph-anchor`, `spring-pop-entrance`, `svg-path-draw`, `reactive-displacement`.
- C: `control-target-sync`, `theme-crossfade-morph`, `card-morph-anchor`, `waterfall-entry`.
- No text disappears merely to return smaller. Reposition and scale persistent groups continuously.
- In the opening hook, each text line holds its position after entering; the group moves only once as the scene exits.
- The opening uses no decorative coral rule and the proof card contains no duplicate `ASÍ`.

## Binding source reuse

- The Claude / ChatGPT / Gemini reveal reuses the original source assets `skill-folder-v1.png`, `claude-window-v1.png`, `chatgpt-window-v1.png` and `gemini-window-v1.png`.
- Its platform-card hierarchy, final geometry, folder placement, connector routes and continuous reveal motion follow the original `STORYBOARD_14-21.md` / `board-14-21` source rather than a visual reinterpretation.
- Native Helvetica labels remain separately editable over the reused raster windows.
